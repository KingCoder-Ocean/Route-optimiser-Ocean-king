        Vans

        Untick a van that’s off the road today. Fuel economy is a starting estimate (set below): put in each vehicle’s real mpg from the fuel card data for accurate fuel and CO₂.

        Planning rules

        Miles are straight-line distances × the road factor, so they’re estimates; “Draw on real roads” on a route measures the real road distance when the routing service can be reached. Loads come from Size × Pack × quantity; lines whose size can’t be read count as zero weight. Changing a setting re-optimises.
